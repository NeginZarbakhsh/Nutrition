Persian food, no scale, 28-day cycle

Your cooking rhythm

Lunch is cooked once and eaten twice. Dinners take 15–20 minutes.

1

Sunday, about an hour

Boil 6 eggs. Wash and dry herbs. Chop cucumber, tomato and onion into boxes. Mix a jar of garlic yogurt (yogurt, crushed garlic, lemon, salt, dried mint). Cook Monday's lunch.

2

Tuesday and Thursday evening

Cook the next lunch, two portions. Split the pot straight into two equal boxes. That split is your measuring.

3

Every other night

Dinner is a quick pan or oven dish. If you are too tired, use the fallback list in the No-scale guide.

Your hand is the scale

Your hand matches your body size, so these portions scale to you. Every amount in the app uses one of these.

Tricks that replace weighing

1

Split the pot, not the portion

Cook the khoresh for exactly 2 portions using the amounts shown, then pour it into 2 equal boxes. No weighing, ever.

2

One mug of raw rice is 4 portions

A normal 250ml mug holds about 200g raw rice. For 2 portions use half a mug. Cooked, one portion is about the size of your fist.

3

Oil with a teaspoon only

Never pour from the bottle. 1 teaspoon = 45 kcal. Four glugs into a pot is about 400 kcal you will not notice. A spray bottle is even better.

4

Count what comes in units

Eggs, pittas, bread slices, salmon fillets, walnuts, pistachios. Counting is as accurate as weighing.

5

Use the pack as the measure

"Half a tin of chickpeas", "a third of the yogurt tub". Open, split, done.

6

The plate rule when eating out

Half the plate vegetables or salad, a palm or two of protein, one fist of rice or one piece of bread. Ask for the oil or sauce on the side.

Free foods, eat as much as you like

Cucumber, tomato, lettuce, herbs and sabzi, radish, spring onion, cabbage, peppers, mushrooms, courgette, aubergine (baked, not fried), torshi, khiar shoor, lemon, sumac, dried mint, spices. Salt-heavy pickles can push the scale up by water overnight. That is not fat.

Your numbers

155 cm. Goal: lose a little fat and keep or build muscle, so the shape gets more defined.

Saturday check-in

Morning, after the bathroom, before breakfast. Compare week to week, never day to day.

What builds the shape

1

Lift heavier over time

Write down weight and reps. Beat one number each session: one more rep, or a little more weight.

2

Protein at every meal

Every meal here carries 25–45g. That spread matters more than a big shake.

3

A small deficit, not a crash

About 1,550 kcal. Eating 1,200 would take muscle with the fat.

4

Sleep 7–8 hours

Short sleep makes you lose more muscle on the same food.

5

Creatine, 3–5g daily

Cheap and well studied. Expect 1–2 kg of water weight in the first two weeks. That is inside the muscle, not fat.
