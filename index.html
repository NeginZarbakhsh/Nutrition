Food you cooked in a big batch

Pasta with chicken, a pot of khoresh, anything you eat from for several days.

1

Note what goes in the pot

Packs and tins, not grams: "1 pack chicken, ½ bag pasta, 1 tin tomatoes, 2 tsp oil". In the app: Change dish → I cooked a big batch.

2

Split into equal boxes straight away

Before it goes in the fridge or freezer. Write the number on the lid: "chicken pasta 1/5". Each box is then exactly one portion.

3

Eat it: tap 1 box

Change dish → your batch. The app knows the protein, and the rest of the day adjusts around it.

4

Already frozen and never tracked?

Pick the closest dish (e.g. Chicken & tomato pasta), tap Ate it → Different amount, and choose the plate size: small, big, very big. Close enough.

Tricks that replace weighing

1

Split the pot, not the portion

Cook for exactly 2 (or 4) portions with the amounts shown, then pour into 2 (or 4) equal boxes.

2

One cup of raw rice is 4 portions

With your measuring cup: 1 cup raw rice is 4 portions, so ½ cup for 2. Dry penne: ⅔ cup per portion, 1⅓ cups for 2.

3

Oil with a teaspoon only

Never pour from the bottle. 1 teaspoon = 45 kcal. Four glugs into a pot is about 400 kcal you will not notice.

0

The app learns your amounts

Change a dish once with "Different amount" (e.g. 3 spoons of oats, milk, 1 tbsp chia) and it is saved as your usual. Next time "Ate it" is enough. To go back, open the dish and tap "Use the plan's amounts again".

4

Count what comes in units

Eggs, wraps, slices, salmon fillets, dates, walnut halves, pistachios. Counting is as accurate as weighing.

5

Eating out or at family dinners

Half the plate salad or sabzi, 4–6 pieces of kabab or chicken, about ¾ cup of cooked rice, and the tahdig is a treat, not a portion.

Free foods, eat as much as you like

Cucumber, tomato, lettuce, herbs and sabzi, radish, spring onion, cabbage, peppers, mushrooms, courgette, spinach, aubergine (baked, not fried), torshi, khiar shoor, lemon, sumac, dried mint, spices, tea. Salty pickles can push the scale up by water overnight. That is not fat.

Garmin sync (iPhone)

Garmin does not let small apps read your account, but your iPhone can pass the number along. Set this up once, about 5 minutes. It works on the GitHub link of this app (neginzarbakhsh.github.io/Nutrition), added to your Home Screen.

1

Let Garmin write to Apple Health

Garmin Connect app → More (☰) → Settings → Connected Apps → Apple Health → turn on Active Calories. Then in the Health app, allow Garmin Connect to write Active Energy.

2

Make the Shortcut

Shortcuts app → + → add these 4 actions:
Find Health Samples where Type is Active Energy and Start Date is Today
Calculate Statistics: Sum of Health Samples
Round Number (Ones place)
Open URLs: https://neginzarbakhsh.github.io/Nutrition/?active= then tap the blue Rounded Number to add it at the end

Name it Sofreh calories.

3

Run it in the evening

Open Garmin Connect first so the watch syncs, then tap the Shortcut. Or let it run itself: Shortcuts → Automation → Time of Day → 7:30pm → Run Sofreh calories.

Active calories are what you burn moving: steps, gym, everything. Your plan already counts a normal day (about the number shown in the Garmin box on Today). You eat back half of anything above that. A very quiet day lowers the target a little, but only after 6pm.
