Tricks that replace weighing

1

Split the pot, not the portion

Cook for exactly 2 (or 4) portions with the amounts shown, then pour into 2 (or 4) equal boxes.

2

One cup of raw rice is 4 portions

With your measuring cup: 1 cup raw rice is 4 portions, so ½ cup for 2. Dry penne: ⅔ cup per portion, 1⅓ cups for 2.

3

Oil with a teaspoon only

Never pour from the bottle. 1 teaspoon = 45 kcal. Four glugs into a pot is about 400 kcal you will not notice.

0

The app learns your amounts

Change a dish once with "Different amount" (e.g. 3 spoons of oats, milk, 1 tbsp chia) and it is saved as your usual. Next time "Ate it" is enough. To go back, open the dish and tap "Use the plan's amounts again".

4

Count what comes in units

Eggs, wraps, slices, salmon fillets, dates, walnut halves, pistachios. Counting is as accurate as weighing.

5

How you get the sporty, toned look

"Toned" is two things at once: enough muscle, and low enough body fat to see it. For most women visible definition starts around 20–24% body fat. The order below is the order of importance.

1

Lift, and lift a bit heavier over time

Your gym classes: Upper Body, Lower Body and Functional each week, with Metabolic swapped in at most once a week. In each class, log the main lifts in the app (weight × reps of your best set) and try to beat them. A strength class every time beats more cardio for the toned look. This is 70% of the result.

2

Protein: about 2 g per kg every day

Around 110g for you, in 4 portions of 25–35g. A protein snack before bed (cottage cheese or skyr) helps overnight repair.

3

Lose fat slowly: 0.25–0.4 kg a week

Faster than that and you lose muscle and strength, and you look flat, not toned. Plan 10–14 weeks of this, then 4–6 weeks at maintenance (eat about 250 kcal more) so your body settles. Then decide: another short cut, or a slow build.

4

Carbs are your training fuel

Rice, oats, potato and fruit before or after lifting. Gym days get 100 kcal more. Do not cut carbs to zero: your lifts and your muscles look better with them.

5

Keep healthy fat above 0.8 g per kg

About 44g a day. Olive oil, nuts, eggs, salmon twice a week. The app never shrinks these when it resizes your meals.

6

Walk 7–10k steps, sleep 7–9 hours

Steps burn more than extra cardio and do not hurt recovery. Short sleep makes you lose muscle instead of fat on the same food, and increases hunger.

7

An easy week every 6–8 weeks

Same exercises, half the sets, lighter weights. You come back stronger. Skipping it is how most plans stall.

Starting running again

Running is great for your heart and mood and helps fat loss. Done right, it does not cost muscle.

1

Start easy

Weeks 1–3: run/walk, e.g. 1 min run + 1 min walk × 10–15. Then 20–30 min easy runs. "Easy" means you can talk in full sentences. Add no more than about 10% a week.

2

Twice a week is enough

Keep your 3 strength classes first. Run on rest days (Tue/Thu/Sat), and not the day before Lower Body, so your legs are fresh for squats.

3

Eat for it

Log the run with "+ Add a run" (distance or Garmin active calories): the app adds about 45% back as carbs. For runs over 45 min, have a banana or toast 30–60 min before.

4

Look after the legs

Proper running shoes (fitted at a running shop), soft surfaces at the start, and stop if a pain gets worse while running. Shin or knee pain means slow down the build-up.

5

Iron and water

Runners lose more iron. If you feel unusually tired or out of breath, ask your GP to check ferritin. Drink 400–600 ml extra on run days.

Your cycle

In the week before your period you may hold 1–2 kg of water, feel hungrier and weaker. That is normal. Keep lifting, use the free snack options, and judge progress by the same week last month. If your period becomes irregular or stops, eat more and see your GP: that is the body saying the deficit is too hard.

Garmin sync (iPhone)

Garmin does not let small apps read your account, but your iPhone can pass the number along. Set this up once, about 5 minutes. It works with the app opened from its web address or Home Screen icon.

1

Let Garmin write to Apple Health

Garmin Connect app → More (☰) → Settings → Connected Apps → Apple Health → turn on Active Calories. Then in the Health app, allow Garmin Connect to write Active Energy.

2

Make the Shortcut

Shortcuts app → + → add these 4 actions:
Find Health Samples where Type is Active Energy, Start Date is Today and Source is Connect (Garmin), so your iPhone's own step counting is left out
Calculate Statistics: Sum of Health Samples
Round Number (Ones place)
Open URLs: this app's address + ?active= then tap the blue Rounded Number to add it at the end

Name it Sofreh calories.

3

Run it after class

After class, open Garmin Connect so the watch syncs, then tap the Shortcut.

You wear the watch only for workouts, so the number is the workout's calories. You eat back about 45% of it; it replaces the class estimate for that day. Your steps the rest of the day are already counted in your plan.
