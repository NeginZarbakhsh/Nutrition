Tricks that replace weighing

1

Split the pot, not the portion

Cook for exactly 2 (or 4) portions with the amounts shown, then pour into 2 (or 4) equal boxes.

2

One mug of raw rice is 4 portions

A 250ml mug holds about 200g raw rice. For 2 portions use half a mug. Dry penne: one mug is 2 portions.

3

Oil with a teaspoon only

Never pour from the bottle. 1 teaspoon = 45 kcal. Four glugs into a pot is about 400 kcal you will not notice.

4

Count what comes in units

Eggs, pittas, slices, salmon fillets, dates, walnut halves, pistachios. Counting is as accurate as weighing.

5

Eating out or at family dinners

Half the plate salad or sabzi, 1–2 palms of kabab or chicken, one fist of rice, and the tahdig is a treat, not a portion.

Free foods, eat as much as you like

Cucumber, tomato, lettuce, herbs and sabzi, radish, spring onion, cabbage, peppers, mushrooms, courgette, spinach, aubergine (baked, not fried), torshi, khiar shoor, lemon, sumac, dried mint, spices, tea. Salty pickles can push the scale up by water overnight. That is not fat.

What builds the shape

1

Lift heavier over time

Write down weight and reps. Beat one number each session.

2

Protein at every meal

25–45g each time. The ring on the Today screen shows how you are doing.

3

A small deficit, not a crash

Your target from the calculator above. Eating 1,200 would take muscle with the fat.

4

Sleep 7–8 hours

Short sleep makes you lose more muscle on the same food.

5

Creatine, 3–5g daily

Cheap and well studied. Expect 1–2 kg of water weight in the first two weeks. That is inside the muscle, not fat.
